This book is a plain-language companion for the **AI Architect** program —
designing, integrating, governing, and defending AI systems across their full
lifecycle.

> An AI Architect is a technical leader who can design, integrate, govern, and
> defend AI-powered systems across their full lifecycle, from a first
> architecture sketch through production, compliance, and the people who have to
> trust it.

## What this book covers

The Architect program has two levels.

**Foundations** — five competencies:

1. Agentic architecture and orchestration
2. Tool design and integration protocols
3. AI coding-assistant configuration and workflows
4. Prompt engineering and structured output
5. Context management and reliability

**Professional** — seven competencies:

1. Solution design and architecture
2. Model selection, prompting, and context engineering
3. Integration
4. Evaluation, testing, and optimization
5. Governance, safety, and risk management
6. Stakeholder communication and lifecycle management
7. Developer productivity and operational enablement

This is the capstone book. It assumes the material in
[AI Fundamentals](/books/fundamentals/), [AI Associate](/books/associate/), and
[AI Developer](/books/developer/).

## How chapters are structured

Plain explanation → worked example → "explain it back" → self-check → key terms.
