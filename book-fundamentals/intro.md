This book is a plain-language companion for the **AI Fundamentals** program. It
assumes no maths and no code. Its job is to make the ideas stick: explain each
concept from first principles, show a worked example, and give you a way to check
whether you actually understood it.

## What "fundamentals" means

> Fundamentals are the essential principles, concepts, and basic skills that form
> the foundation of a subject, system, or practice.

**AI Fundamentals**, then, is the groundwork: what artificial intelligence is,
how it works in broad strokes, why it behaves the way it does, and how the pieces
of the landscape fit together — enough to reason about AI, not just use it.

## Why the book begins with humans

> To understand artificial intelligence, you first need a clear point of
> comparison: human beings. Before this book explains what AI is, it begins with
> what makes a person a person — because human beings and human intelligence are
> the baseline against which AI is understood.

Part 1 builds that baseline. Part 2 uses it to explain AI.

## How this book is structured

Each chapter follows the same shape:

1. **The plain explanation** — the idea from first principles, in everyday words.
2. **A worked example** — one concrete case, reasoned through step by step.
3. **Now explain it back** — a prompt to teach the idea to someone else. Where you
   get stuck is what to re-read.
4. **Self-check** — a few questions with collapsible answers.
5. **Key terms** — short definitions you can return to.

## Where this fits

`AI Fundamentals → AI Associate → AI Developer → AI Architect`

Fundamentals is where you *understand* AI. The later books are about applying it
to professional work, building AI-enabled applications, and designing production
systems.
